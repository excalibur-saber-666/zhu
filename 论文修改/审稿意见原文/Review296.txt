This paper proposes a two-sided CUSUM-based weighted sliding-window factor graph optimization method for multi-UAV cooperative navigation under intermittent NLOS interference. An alpha-beta predictor on each leader-follower range edge generates signed normalized innovations for CUSUM monitoring, and the resulting weights are integrated into a sliding-window factor graph with feedback to an error-state Kalman filter. 
1. The paper should clarify how the CUSUM threshold and drift parameters are selected and how sensitive the performance is to these choices.
2. The authors should include additional scenarios with different formation sizes and varying NLOS intensity to strengthen the generality of the conclusions.
3. The selective isolation mechanism after alarm confirmation is described but not analyzed in detail, particularly regarding the risk of isolating healthy edges or failing to recover them promptly. 
4. The comparison focuses mainly on positioning error metrics, while other relevant aspects such as computational cost, convergence behavior, and real-time feasibility are not reported.
5. Several figures are referenced without sufficient interpretation, and the reproduction of the method's pipeline is partly obscured by unclear formatting in the rendered text.
